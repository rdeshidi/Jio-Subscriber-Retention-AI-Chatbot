INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A redemption-response propensity model may be feasible if there are sufficient redeemers and non-redeemers among exposed customers.
However, a proper causal uplift model requires defensible treatment/control assignment and treatment timing.
Simple churn differences between exposed and unexposed customers must not be reported as the causal effect of discounts.
The next modelling step should be chosen from the actual feasibility results rather than forcing uplift modelling when the required experimental structure is absent.
