QUESTION
Which 5 circles have the highest 30-day churn rate?

VERIFIED ANSWER
Top 5 circles by 30-day churn rate:
- Uttar Pradesh East: 2.77%
- Madhya Pradesh & Chhattisgarh: 2.49%
- Bihar & Jharkhand: 2.44%
- Uttar Pradesh West: 2.40%
- Assam: 2.37%

SQL used:
SELECT
    circle,
    COUNT(*) AS total_subscribers,
    SUM(churn_flag_30d) AS churned_subscribers,
    ROUND(AVG(churn_flag_30d) * 100, 2) AS churn_rate_percentage
FROM subscribers
GROUP BY circle
ORDER BY churn_rate_percentage DESC
LIMIT 5;
