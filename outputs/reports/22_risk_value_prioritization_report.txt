JIO SUBSCRIBER RETENTION PROJECT
DAY 2 - STEP 22 RISK-VALUE PRIORITISATION
====================================================================================================

PURPOSE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The CLV assessment found no observed future-value target.
Therefore the project uses a transparent 12-month ARPU-based revenue proxy together with the frozen churn model's risk ranking.

RISK-VALUE PRIORITY INDEX
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
risk percentile x value percentile x 100

The index is unitless.
It is NOT an estimated rupee loss and should not be interpreted as a calibrated financial expectation.

INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Priority 1 identifies subscribers who are simultaneously in the highest churn-risk decile and highest customer-value quartile.
Priority 2 captures highest-risk subscribers with upper-mid customer value.
Priority 3 contains other subscribers in the highest-risk decile.
Priority 4 creates a watchlist of high-value customers within the top three risk deciles.
All other customers remain under standard monitoring.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The CatBoost model was trained with class weighting, so churn scores are used here primarily for ranking rather than interpreted as perfectly calibrated real-world probabilities.
Similarly, the 12-month revenue measure is an ARPU-based proxy, not observed lifetime contribution or profit.
