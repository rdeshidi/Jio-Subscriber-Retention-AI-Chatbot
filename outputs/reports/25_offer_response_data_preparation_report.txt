IMPORTANT LIMITATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The dataset does not provide timestamps showing whether every predictor was measured before the historical offer exposure.
Therefore the response model is suitable for sandbox analysis and propensity demonstration, but not yet a production causal targeting model.
