CLV TARGET ASSESSMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
No genuine observed future CLV, future contribution, future margin, or next-12-month revenue target was found in the subscriber dataset.

DECISION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A supervised CLV regression target will NOT be invented.
Instead, this sandbox stage creates a transparent 12-month revenue proxy for customer-value prioritisation.

PROXY DEFINITION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Preferred monthly value basis:
1. 6-month average ARPU
2. 3-month average ARPU when 6-month ARPU is unavailable
3. Last-month ARPU when both averages are unavailable

12-month revenue proxy = monthly value basis x 12

This is an annualized revenue proxy, not realized customer lifetime value.

LIMITATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The supplied dataset does not contain observed future 12-month revenue or contribution margin.
Therefore the current proxy should be used for value segmentation and prioritisation, not presented as an observed financial CLV outcome.
Future churn risk and retention economics can be combined with this value proxy in a later risk-value prioritisation stage.
